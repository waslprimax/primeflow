AI for the procurement of large organisations

Procurement where people decide and AI does the routine

We are a supplier and go through the tenders of large companies every day. That is why we build AI agents that take the most time-consuming work off procurement teams.

01 · The pain

A familiar picture

An ordinary day in a procurement department.

Specifications dozens of pages long

A specialist reads requirements by hand, copies items into spreadsheets and checks them against supplier offers.

Documents checked by eye

Certificates, passports, company details, validity dates — hundreds of details where one missed line becomes a claim.

Deadlines kept in people's heads

Deliveries, payments and replies are tracked through email, spreadsheets and personal reminders.

Decisions that are hard to trace

Why this supplier, who approved it and on what basis — during an audit it is pieced together.

02 · The cost

What it costs

Routine does not look expensive until you count the consequences.

Missed deadlines

One late delivery stops a repair or production line and brings penalties.

Repeat procurements

An error in a specification or document means running the procedure again and losing weeks.

Risk and audits

Opaque decisions invite abuse and difficult conversations with auditors.

Team burnout

Strong specialists spend the day copying instead of negotiating and saving budget.

AI agents inside your process
